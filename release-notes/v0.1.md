> [!NOTE]
> The first release of Hotaru, an early beta. It installs on a TV in Developer Mode (or one rooted with the Homebrew
> Channel), not from the LG Content Store.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Built and tested on one TV, an LG 50UP75003LF on webOS 6: other TVs are untested. On webOS the DualSense has no
> rumble and no touchpad touches, which its driver hides.

Hotaru brings PlayStation Remote Play to LG webOS TVs, built on
[chiaki-ng](https://github.com/streetpea/chiaki-ng)'s libchiaki.

### New Features

- Remote Play from a PS5 or a PS4, on the local network or over PSN: the console is reached through PSN's NAT
  traversal when you are away (a0c66a4)
- Hardware decoding of H.264 and H.265, with HDR10 on a PS5, and 48 kHz audio through the TV's own media pipeline
  (a0c66a4)
- Smooth playback: a short playout buffer at an even cadence hides the stalls a TV's Wi-Fi causes (a0c66a4)
- Pair a console with its PIN, or sign in with your PSN account once on the phone and let the TV keep the tokens and
  bring in the consoles on the account (f0815ff)
- Home built around your consoles: discovery wakes a console in rest mode before connecting, follows it when the
  router moves its address, and shows the controller's battery (740269b)
- A menu over the running game, with the stream quality and the route the console is reached on (a0c66a4)
- Everything is driven by the DualShock 4, the DualSense or the TV remote, with no pointer needed (a0c66a4)
- A startup splash, and a notice when a newer release is out (7763ffa, 9e75c76)
- Releases carry `com.renjfk.hotaru.manifest.json`, the manifest Dev Manager and the Homebrew Channel read (83a2c18)
