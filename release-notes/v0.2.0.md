### New Features

- Stream presets in Settings: **Data saver**, **Balanced** and **High quality** set the resolution, frame rate and
  bitrate together, and **Custom** shows the separate Quality and Bitrate rows for a setting of your own (b6f6a94)
- The menu over the game shows the live video rate and the frame rate the console is sending beside the session
  clock, so a stutter can be told from a setting that is too high (b6f6a94)
- Ending a stream can put the console in rest mode: the **Disconnect** card says whether the console will stay on
  or go to sleep, and the choice is remembered between sessions (28d0730)

### Improvements

- The stream menu's cards change in place: **L1/R1** step the focused card, or the remote's Up/Down when no
  controller is connected, and the hints under the cards follow what the focused card can do. Cross on **Resume**
  goes back to the game and Cross on **Disconnect** ends the stream at once, with no adjust mode to enter and
  leave first (2592a8a)
